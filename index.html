3D transition concept

The floating steak and ham react to mouse movement and scroll position.

The current build uses real-time Three.js geometry as a lightweight browser-native mockup. Later we can replace these with generated GLB/GLTF models from a 3D workflow while keeping the same transition controls.

  1. Hover/mouse move: cuts rotate and “present” the next section.
  2. Scroll: color, scale, and position shift between meat, seafood, ordering, and AI surfaces.
  3. Reduced motion: users who prefer less motion get a calmer layout.
